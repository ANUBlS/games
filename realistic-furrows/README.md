# Realistic Furrows of Time

A more realistic first-person farming simulation inspired by Furrows of Time.

This version focuses on visual and physical realism:

- Dynamic terrain with actual furrows that form when you plow
- Organic crop models that grow over time
- Physically-based lighting and shadows
- Soil and vegetation materials
- Weather and time-of-day effects (basic)
- Same core progression across ages, but with refined mechanics

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Requires internet for three.js CDN.

Controls are similar: WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to toggle working, E near barns to sell.

## Differences from original

- Smaller but denser field for higher visual fidelity
- Terrain deformation for plowing
- Better crop representation
- Improved atmosphere

Progress saves in localStorage.
